1. Controller

I, Maik Walter, am responsible for this website. My postal address is provided in the legal notice. For data protection questions, you can contact maik3531@gmail.com.

2. Hosting through GitHub Pages

The website is served through GitHub Pages. When you access it, GitHub receives technically necessary connection data, including your IP address and information about the requested file. GitHub confirms that it logs and stores IP addresses for security purposes, even if you are not signed in to GitHub. See GitHub's information on data collection by Pages.

Hosting serves the reliable provision and security of this website. These legitimate interests form the basis for the technical provision I arrange under Art. 6 Abs. 1 Buchst. f DSGVO. The website cannot be served without the transmission of the data necessary for the connection.

GitHub identifies its providers as GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, Niederlande, and GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Data may also be processed in the USA and other countries. For such transfers, GitHub describes EU standard contractual clauses, among other mechanisms, as a safeguard.

GitHub does not specify a fixed deletion period for Pages connection data in the linked information. According to GitHub, retention depends on the purpose of processing and legal obligations. Further details about recipients, transfers and retention are provided in GitHub's privacy statement; its privacy contact is privacy@github.com.

3. Website features and embedded media

The website code does not set cookies, use Local Storage or comparable persistent browser storage of its own, or contain analytics or advertising trackers. There is no registration and no contact form.

Images, videos, subtitles, stylesheets and JavaScript are loaded through the same GitHub Pages hosting. No third-party video players, external font services or social media widgets are embedded. Loading video metadata and playing a video also generate requests to the hosting provider.

The share button copies a prepared text containing the page address to the clipboard only when you click it. It does not read existing clipboard content or transmit the text to a social service. The print button opens your browser's print function.

When you visit the general home address, a script uses the preferred languages configured in your browser to select a suitable available language entry page. An explicitly selected language address takes precedence. The selection takes place in your browser and is not sent to an additional service. I do not set cookies for this purpose or save the language selection in Local Storage or any other persistent browser storage.

4. Links to other providers

Some downloads, source code, feedback channels and press sources are linked on other websites. If you visit one of these destination pages, its provider processes connection data and, where applicable, other data under its own privacy notices. Depending on your browser and settings, the referring page may also be transmitted.

The links do not automatically embed these external offerings in the Magnolie page. Legal information about the links is provided in the legal notice.

5. Contact by email

If you write to me, I process your sender address, your message and any information you voluntarily provide in it to deal with your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 Abs. 1 Buchst. f DSGVO; my legitimate interest is responding to enquiries about the project. I cannot reply without a sender address at which you can be reached.

The stated mailbox uses Gmail. Google processes messages and connection data in this context. The provider for users in the European Economic Area is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Irland. Processing outside the European Economic Area is possible. Google refers to adequacy mechanisms, including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and, where these do not apply, standard contractual clauses. Google's privacy policy and its information on international data transfers explain this in more detail and provide a contact for copies of the clauses.

Correspondence is deleted once it is no longer necessary to deal with the enquiry and any follow-up questions. This does not affect statutory retention obligations or retention necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

6. Your rights

Subject to the statutory conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability (Art. 15–20 DSGVO).

Right to object: Where processing is based on Art. 6 Abs. 1 Buchst. f DSGVO, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 DSGVO). Please use the contact details above for this purpose.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your habitual residence, place of work or the alleged infringement (Art. 77 DSGVO).

In connection with this website, I do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects, nor do I carry out corresponding profiling.
