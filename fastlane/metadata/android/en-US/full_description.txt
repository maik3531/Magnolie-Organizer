Magnolie Notes is a local notebook and task list for Android. Organize notes in notebooks, attach images or PDF files, and import text shared by other apps.

Features:
* Notes with creation and modification dates, notebooks and attachments.
* Tasks with due dates, priorities and optional reminders.
* Import shared text and supported exported note files.
* Local encrypted storage and user-controlled backups.
* Optional encrypted sharing with paired Magnolie Organizer installations on Linux and Windows.
* Twenty interface languages.

No account or cloud service is required to use the notebook. Magnolie Organizer is a separate, free desktop application.

Optional connections and permissions:
* Discover Organizer or Magnolienbaum devices on the local network using UDP broadcast and Android network service discovery (NSD/mDNS). Discovery announces connection details; it does not authorize another device to read your notes. Pairing requires confirmation.
* Share and synchronize selected notes and tasks with paired devices over encrypted connections. Bluetooth/RFCOMM can be enabled as a data fallback when the operating system and the paired devices support it; automatic Personal Sync remains Wi-Fi-only.
* Allow a paired desktop to request outgoing calls on this phone, show incoming-call information, and request answering or ending a call. These actions depend on Android permissions and system restrictions. Call-related permissions include phone state, phone-number access and, where Android requires it for caller identification, call-log access.
* Read Android contacts and their available photos, import reviewed contact cards, or synchronize contacts with a permitted branch. Contact writing needs its own Android permission. Read-only desktop access does not write phone contacts.
* Optionally mirror the titles and text of notifications from apps you choose to the paired desktop. This requires enabling Magnolie Notes in Android’s notification-access settings and selecting the apps to share; it does not grant unrestricted access to those apps’ private data.

These integrations are opt-in through pairing, feature choices and the relevant Android permission or access prompts. They are not required for local note-taking. You can disable a feature, revoke its permission or remove a paired device. Bluetooth data transport is separate from any system-managed hands-free call-audio connection. Magnolie Notes does not provide native SMS or RCS messaging.
